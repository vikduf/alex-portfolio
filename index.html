Product Launch

Roll & Glow: Huile Prodigieuse Roll-On Launch

Planned and delivered the UK launch campaign for NUXE's Huile Prodigieuse Roll-On, opening at John Lewis HQ as part of an activation that travelled around the UK. The launch was built to put the product in people's hands: a "Roll & Glow" sampling cart with branded quiz bottles, a custom gold "Spin to Win" wheel with integrated shelving, hero product dummies under a branded arch, and branded FSDUs. Every piece was briefed, costed and fabricated with suppliers, then installed on site.

  • Launch plan across multiple consumer touchpoints
  • Sampling cart & branded quiz bottles
  • Custom FSDU with interactive spin wheel
  • Budget, suppliers & installation on site

About

I'm Alexandra, a brand and trade marketer who takes products from first brief to the shelf, then reads the results to make the next launch better. Whatever the project, the principles stay the same: understand what makes people choose, tell a clear story, deliver on time and on budget.

Over three years as Trade Marketing Executive at NUXE in London, I led seasonal product and packaging projects (Christmas gift sets, custom pouches, gifts with purchase) from creative and supplier briefs, through quotes, costs and timelines, to rollout across Boots, M&S and John Lewis. I built the annual trade marketing strategy, managed a budget of around €100,000, and produced weekly and monthly sell-out analyses that turned data into recommendations for future launches. I also trained and supervised an intern for a full year.

Earlier roles at F4A and Lubrizol added market research, competitor benchmarking, focus groups and the launch of a sustainable personal care range. Today I work at Essembl, a Luxembourg start-up with a consumer app of 2.5 million users, where I help set up the processes that support its growth.

Food has long been one of the sectors I most wanted to work in. As a keen cook, I'm naturally curious about what makes people choose one product over another, and I'm now looking for a brand role where I can bring that curiosity to products people enjoy every day.
