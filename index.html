Product Launch Activation

Roll & Glow — Huile Prodigieuse Roll-On Launch

Designed and produced a large-scale multi-touchpoint activation for the UK launch of NUXE's Huile Prodigieuse Roll-On at John Lewis HQ. The space included a custom-built gold "Spin to Win" wheel with integrated product shelving, two dummies of the hero products displayed under a branded arch, a "Roll & Glow" sampling cart with NUXE-branded quiz bottles, and branded FSDU displays — all fabricated to spec with suppliers and installed on-site. This set-up was part of a wider activation that moved within the UK.

  • Custom FSDU with interactive spin wheel
  • Hero product dummies under branded arch
  • Branded sampling cart & quiz bottles
  • Full supplier coordination & on-site build

About

I'm Alexandra, a hands-on creative who turns abstract briefs into physical retail environments that customers want to walk into, touch, and buy from. Whether it's a skincare gondola or a food hall display, the principles are the same: guide the eye, tell a story, drive the sale.

With 3.5+ years at NUXE as a Trade Marketing Executive, I've owned the full A-to-Z lifecycle of retail projects: from initial concept and mood-boarding, through budgeting, supplier coordination and manufacturer briefing, to on-site installation and rollout across 500+ stores at retailers including Boots, M&S, and ASOS.

My work includes planogram design, custom gondola and FSDU creation, experiential activations, seasonal campaigns, and press events. I'm now looking to bring this expertise into premium Food & Beverage, grocery, and hospitality industries where spatial design and product storytelling matter just as much.
