Product Launch Activation

Roll & Glow: Huile Prodigieuse Roll-On Launch

Planned and delivered a large launch campaign with multiple touchpoints for NUXE's Huile Prodigieuse Roll-On in the UK, opening at John Lewis HQ. The space included a custom gold "Spin to Win" wheel with integrated product shelving, two dummies of the hero products displayed under a branded arch, a "Roll & Glow" sampling cart with branded NUXE quiz bottles, and branded FSDU displays. Everything was fabricated to spec with suppliers and installed on site. This setup was part of a wider activation that travelled around the UK.

  • Custom FSDU with interactive spin wheel
  • Hero product dummies under branded arch
  • Branded sampling cart & quiz bottles
  • Budget, suppliers & installation on site

About

I'm Alexandra, a hands-on marketer who turns a brief into something real: a launch, a campaign, an event, a display that customers notice and act on. Whatever the format, the principles are the same: know the audience, tell a clear story, deliver on time and on budget.

With 3.5+ years at NUXE as a Trade Marketing Executive, I built the annual trade marketing plan and budget and owned every campaign A to Z: from brief, concept and mood boards, through budgeting, supplier coordination and manufacturer briefing, to launch and rollout across 500+ stores at retailers including Boots, M&S, and ASOS. Alongside the creative work I produced weekly and monthly sales reports and analysis to guide commercial decisions.

My work spans product launches, seasonal campaigns, experiential activations, press events, packaging and point of sale design. Earlier marketing roles at F4A and Lubrizol added market research, competitor benchmarking, social content and agency collaboration to the mix. I'm now looking for a marketing role where I can own projects end to end and see ideas through to results.
